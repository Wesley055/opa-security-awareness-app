### Enrollment - do not start until D&B activation completes

**Both programs verify against the D&B RECORD, not the number.** Enrolling inside
the 2-3 day activation window is the classic cause of a rejection you then have to
appeal.

**Apple Developer Program (~$99/yr).** Enroll from the Apple Developer app on an
iPhone/iPad/Mac with biometrics - **same device throughout**, Apple Account with
2FA on. Enter your **legal** name, not a nickname or the company name. Entity type
**Organization**. Supply D-U-N-S, HQ address, an **answerable** phone, and the
website. Confirm signing authority. **Then take the verification call.**

**Google Play (~$25 one-time).** Choose **Organization**, not Personal -
organization accounts also avoid the closed-testing gate (12 testers for 14 days)
that new personal accounts hit. Link a Google Payments profile (**required even
for a free app**). Enter the D-U-N-S up front. Verify contact email, contact
phone, developer email and developer phone by OTP. Have the CAC certificate ready.

### Traps for this track

- **A `noindex` meta on `/robots.txt` before the fix was NOT a problem.** Next.js
  served its 404 page and always marks 404s `noindex`.
- **Commit is not deploy.**
- **A live site is not a discoverable site.** Fetching succeeds while searching
  fails, and verifiers search.
- **`apps/website` has no tests** - gate on `npm run build`, which proves
  compilation, not rendering.
- **`middleware` is deprecated in Next 16.2 in favour of `proxy`.**
- **The phone must be answered Mon-Fri 9:00-5:00 WAT.** Apple calls it. An
  unanswered line is a failed enrollment, not a retry.
- **A store build is a custom dev build**, so trap #75 applies - **fix `app.json`
  before any EAS work, or the submitted build ships an SOS button that cannot get
  a location.**
