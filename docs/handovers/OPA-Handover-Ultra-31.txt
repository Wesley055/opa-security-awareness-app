## 1. WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY
================================================================================

[M] Market feedback indicated a Command Center sold to HOSPITALS would not
sell in Nigeria. The reasoning holds independently of the market: a hospital
is not staffed to watch a screen for incoming alerts - a triage nurse is
triaging. A DASHBOARD NOBODY WATCHES IS WORSE THAN AN SMS, because at least an
SMS interrupts.

[DECIDED] THE CUSTOMER WAS NARROWED, NOT THE FEATURE SET.

  PRIMARY    private security companies - staffed control rooms, multiple
             clients, already watching for incidents
  PRIMARY    gated estates - the gatehouse has someone at a desk
  EXTENSIBLE universities, corporate SOC, from the same model
  LATER      ambulance and response companies, when incidents escalate to them
  NOT A      hospitals. They are DESTINATIONS, reached by message, not
  DASHBOARD  dashboard users with accounts
  ALREADY    families - served by SMS and the tracking link
  SERVED

[DECIDED] SECOND AND LARGER CHANGE: THE CONSUMER APP DOES NOT WAIT FOR ANY OF
THIS. A controlled beta of 10-20 KNOWN users starts as soon as contact routing
is trustworthy. Command Center is built IN PARALLEL.

[R] The commercial reason is stronger than the speed one. Approaching an
estate with "your residents already use OPA to alert their families - connect
your gatehouse and those same emergencies appear at your security desk" is a
far easier conversation than selling an empty dashboard with fabricated
incidents. Consumer adoption becomes the B2B demonstration.

[R] THE RISK THIS AVOIDS. Ultra 30 planned ten to fourteen days of Command
Center UI built entirely on an untested market assumption. Two internal
documents already described a system that did not exist; building on an
untested assumption is the same error at a larger scale.


================================================================================
## 2. THE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE - FROZEN
================================================================================

[DECIDED]

  OPA does not monetise by withholding the minimum emergency capability
  needed to keep a person safe. OPA monetises additional protection,
  organisational response, intelligence, accountability and scale.

Binding consequences:

- The SOS button, location capture, contact alerting and the tracking link are
  FREE and MUST ACTUALLY WORK. A deliberately weakened free tier would make
  market validation meaningless and is indefensible in a safety product.
- What is sold is the ORGANISATIONAL layer: facility routing, centralised
  incidents, operator acknowledgement, simultaneous incidents, response
  accountability, history, reports, SafeWalk policies, escalation rules,
  operator roles.
- An individual cannot reproduce that by having a free app that sends an SOS.

## 5. COMMAND CENTER - SCOPE FROZEN, NO CODE
================================================================================

[DECIDED] Built IN PARALLEL once the beta is running. Not before, not as a
prerequisite for it.

### 5.1 The loop, and nothing else

    SOS -> facility security desk receives it -> operator ACKNOWLEDGES
        -> watches live location and timeline
        -> coordinates through their OWN radios and procedures
        -> the USER closes with I'm Safe / False Alarm

[DECIDED] ROLES:
  - The USER owns the emergency outcome. Resolve and cancel stay OWNER-ONLY.
  - The OPERATOR owns acknowledgement and response coordination.
  - OPA owns telemetry, audit, delivery, identity and software.

[R] DO NOT GIVE THE OPERATOR A RESOLVE BUTTON because they happen to be
sitting at the screen. ADR-013 section 6.2.

[DECIDED] Physical dispatch stays OUTSIDE the MVP. Security companies keep
using their radios, phones and procedures. No dispatch engine, no hospital
workflow, no speculative AI.

### 5.2 Membership and routing - all DECIDED, none BUILT

  Membership          resident joins a facility with an estate-issued
                      INVITE CODE
  Cardinality         User.facilityId is 0 or 1 facility. DELIBERATE MVP LIMIT
  Incident ownership  SNAPSHOT User.facilityId -> Incident.facilityId at
                      creation. Never derive historical access from current
                      membership
  Routing             facility security AND nominated contacts. The facility
                      does NOT replace family
  Authorization       operators see only incidents whose Incident.facilityId
                      matches theirs
  Revocation          revoking a code stops new enrolment; existing residents
                      stay
  Leaving             a separate operation. Affects future incidents, not
                      historical ones
  Codes               random, revocable, optionally expiring. NOT PINNOCK2026

[R] WRITE THE FUTURE MIGRATION DOWN BEFORE DESIGNING ANY API:
      MVP    User.facilityId
      Later  UserFacilityMembership(userId, facilityId, role, status, ...)
    Otherwise endpoints will assume one facility is a permanent business rule.

[DECIDED] OPERATOR ALERTING: SHORT POLLING every 3-5 seconds for the pilot,
with a local visual and audio alarm on a new OPEN incident. Not SSE, not
WebSocket - polling is simple, observable, easy to debug, and can be replaced
later without touching the incident model.

[DECIDED] UNATTENDED SCREEN: a FACILITY BACKUP ALERT to a designated security
phone number by SMS. A gatehouse at 3am may have nobody at the desk, and the
answer must not be "nothing happens".

[R] Facility.phoneNumber EXISTS in the schema, but the orchestrator fans out
only to EmergencyContact rows belonging to a USER. A facility recipient is a
NEW RECIPIENT TYPE - small, but not free.

That keeps the sales language honest: Command Center open means a
near-real-time alarm and live view; Command Center closed means the facility
security phone still receives the alert; nominated contacts are alerted
independently either way.

### 5.3 What exists, measured 7 August

[BUILT] UserRole, User.facilityId, Facility, Incident.facilityId,
FacilityStaffGuard (re-reads role and facilityId from the DATABASE rather than
trusting the token), IncidentAccessGuard,
GET /facilities/:facilityId/incidents, and the public tracking page proving

[NOT BUILT] The entire operator UI. Facility and staff PROVISIONING -

- facilities.service.ts:8-25 returns every incident with all notifications,
evidence and timeline events, unbounded.

[NOT BUILT] FACILITY ROUTING ITSELF. Incident.facilityId exists and NOTHING
SETS IT. "Resident triggers SOS -> security desk receives it" has no mechanism
today. This is the P0 that was missing from every earlier list, and it is
larger than acknowledgement.

[R] TENANT ISOLATION IS NOT NEGOTIABLE. Estate A must never see Estate B's
residents or incidents. The guards exist; provisioning does not. Retrofitting
tenancy after customers hold real incident data is dangerous. VERIFY ISOLATION
FOR EVERY CUSTOMER ONBOARDED - log in as A, confirm B is invisible. It is the
easiest check to assume and the worst to get wrong.

## 6. THE PLATFORM MODEL - DIRECTION, MOSTLY NOT BUILT
================================================================================

Protect -> Detect -> Alert -> Acknowledge -> Track -> Coordinate -> Close ->
Report -> Audit

  Protect       SafeWalk. Journey sessions and tracking [BUILT].
                Check-ins [NOT BUILT]
  Detect        SOS [BUILT]. Voice [NOT BUILT]. Journey Intelligence
                [NOT BUILT]
  Alert         orchestrator [BUILT]. SMS live. WhatsApp/Push/Voice are stubs
  Acknowledge   Command Center [NOT BUILT]
  Track         journey + tracking link [BUILT]
  Coordinate    the customer's own radios and procedures - DELIBERATELY NOT
                OPA'S
  Close         user, owner-only [BUILT and VERIFIED 7 August]
  Report        [NOT BUILT]. Mostly a projection over data that already exists
  Audit         timeline hash chain [BUILT and VERIFIED]

[DECIDED] THREE DETECTION CHANNELS CONVERGE ON ONE INCIDENT PIPELINE - manual
(SOS), voice (on-device keyword), behavioural (Journey Intelligence). This is
the strongest architectural idea from the strategy session and belongs in the
white paper as OPA MULTI-MODAL EMERGENCY DETECTION.

[R] STATE IT PRECISELY. ONLY SOS EXISTS. The other two are roadmap.

### 6.1 Report vs Audit

[DECIDED]
  AUDIT  = tamper-evident evidence of what OPA OBSERVED
  REPORT = a human-readable operational account GENERATED FROM that evidence

Operators may add notes. OPERATOR ASSERTIONS MUST NEVER BECOME OBSERVED SYSTEM
FACTS. "Guard Bravo dispatched at 22:17" is an operator claim; the chain must
not absorb it. Same boundary as ADR-013 section 6.2, applied to a new surface.

[R] The report is closer than it looks - most fields already exist on the
incident, the journey chain, the notification rows and the timeline.
Acknowledgement time is the one that does not, and it is already P0 for
Command Center.

### 6.2 SafeWalk check-ins

[DECIDED] SAFEWALK SCHEDULES. JOURNEY INTELLIGENCE INTERPRETS.

    SafeWalk started
      -> 30 min -> "Are you safe?"
           -> YES -> continue
           -> no response -> grace period -> prompt again
                -> still nothing -> JOURNEY INTELLIGENCE
                     -> escalation policy -> contacts and/or incident

[DECIDED] A MISSED CHECK-IN IS NOT AN SOS. People drive, sleep, lose signal,
miss notifications. OPA MUST NOT MANUFACTURE EMERGENCIES FROM ORDINARY
BEHAVIOUR - that is a design constraint, not a caveat.

The defensible customer claim: "During an active lone-worker SafeWalk, OPA
requests a safety confirmation every 30 minutes. Missed confirmations follow
the organisation's configured escalation procedure." NOT "OPA knows when
someone is in danger."

[DECIDED] JOURNEY INTELLIGENCE SIGNAL #1 IS PROLONGED SILENCE. Not route
deviation - that needs an expected-route model that does not exist. Silence
builds directly on existing journey infrastructure.

[R] DEPENDENCY NOBODY HAD NAMED: CHECK-INS NEED PUSH, AND PUSH IS A STUB.
"Are you safe?" has to reach the phone. That puts the push provider on the
SafeWalk critical path, not just the honesty list.

### 6.3 Voice / Picovoice

[DECIDED] Belongs to Detect. Does not block the beta or the Command Center
MVP.

[R] ITS HONEST SCOPE IS NARROW: on-device recognition of an explicit
configured distress keyword. It is NOT scream detection, emotion detection,
tone analysis, or general interpretation of conversation. "Voice detection"
invites the larger reading and a security director will ask.

[R] DO NOT BUY THE PICOVOICE TRIAL until integration is about to start. A
trial clock that expires unused is money gone, and nothing in the beta or the
Command Center loop needs it.
